confidant·e Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

confidant·e is made by Nader Amir ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

We collect nothing

confidant·e has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not receive your text, your questions, your dictation, your API key or any information about how you use the app.

What stays on your Mac

Your drafts, conversations, memory notes, ratings, saved questions and settings are stored on your Mac in the app's own storage. Files you save from the app go wherever you choose to put them. Downloaded AI models are stored on your Mac. If you add a Claude API key, it is stored in your Mac's Keychain.

When information leaves your Mac

Text you type or paste is never sent anywhere unless you choose an online AI. Information leaves your Mac only in these cases, each of which follows from something you do:

  • Downloading a model. When you download the built-in model or a dictation model, the app fetches the file from the service that hosts it (Hugging Face, or Ollama's library if you use Ollama). That service sees a normal download request, including your IP address. Your text is not sent.
  • Using Claude. If you select Claude and enter your own Anthropic API key, the text you clean up or ask about, together with your memory notes when you ask a question, is sent directly from your Mac to Anthropic, and is handled under Anthropic's terms and privacy policy. We never see it.
  • Using Apple dictation. Dictation uses Apple dictation unless you choose otherwise. Your speech is then processed by Apple's speech service and may be sent to Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. If you choose the Whisper option instead, your speech is transcribed on your Mac and is not sent anywhere.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone only while you are dictating. We never receive or store the audio.

Children

confidant·e is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
