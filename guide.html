confidant·e user guide

confidant·e helps you write and lets you ask an AI questions, with the AI running on your own Mac if you want it to. This guide covers everything in the app. Each section matches one of the tutorial videos in videos/.

1. Why use it

Listen: An introduction, recorded by Nader Amir.

Most AI writing tools send what you type to a company's servers. That is fine for a birthday message. It is not fine for a letter to your doctor, a note about a client, a draft about a colleague, or a question you would rather nobody else saw.

confidant·e can do the same work with an AI that runs on your Mac:

  • Your words stay with you. With a local AI, nothing you write or ask leaves the computer.
  • It works offline. Once the AI is downloaded, you do not need the internet.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.
  • You choose. When you want a stronger online AI and do not mind sharing, you can switch to Claude for that one job.

2. What you need

To use You need
The built-in AI A Mac with macOS 14 or later, 16 GB of memory, and about 2.5 GB of free disk space for a one-time download
Larger local AIs The free Ollama app, and more memory: 32 to 64 GB for mid-sized models, 128 GB recommended for the most powerful
Claude (online) An internet connection and your own Anthropic API key, which Anthropic bills for what you send

3. Getting started

Listen: Getting started, recorded by Nader Amir.

  1. Open confidant·e. An opening screen with the app's name shows for a moment, then the welcome page. Read it, then press Get started.
  2. If no AI is ready on your Mac, the app offers to download the built-in one. Press Download. It is about 2.5 GB and only happens once.
  3. A bar at the top shows the download's progress. When it finishes, the built-in AI is selected for you.

If you dismissed the offer, open the Clean up with menu at the top and choose Built-in Gemma 3 4B … · download.

To see the welcome page again, open Help at the top right of the window and choose Welcome page. The same menu has this guide, the tutorials and support. Untick Show this when the app opens on that page if you do not want it at every launch. The opening screen has the same tick box.

4. Clean up your writing

The app opens in Clean up mode, with two boxes side by side.

  1. Type or paste your rough text into Your text on the left.
  2. Press Clean up (or Command-Return).
  3. The corrected version appears in Cleaned up on the right.

The AI fixes spelling, grammar and punctuation and smooths awkward sentences. It keeps your meaning, your tone and your paragraphs.

While it works, a line under the boxes shows a timer and what is happening. The first run after opening the app is slower because the AI has to load into memory. When it finishes, the line shows how long it took.

After a clean-up, the words the AI changed or added are shaded in the right-hand box, and a label above it counts them, such as "12 changes". Click the label to hide or show the shading. Words that were only removed leave no mark.

What you can do with the result:

  • Copy puts it on the clipboard.
  • Use as my text moves it to the left box so you can keep editing and clean up again.
  • Save to file saves it as a file (see section 9).
  • Stop ends a run early if it is going wrong.

You can edit the cleaned-up text directly in the right-hand box. Always read it before you send it: an AI can change a meaning without warning.

5. Dictate instead of typing

  1. Press Dictate. The first time, macOS asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Allow both.
  2. Speak. Your words appear in Your text, after anything already there.
  3. Press Stop dictating when you are done, then Clean up to tidy what you said.

The Dictate with menu at the top chooses how speech is recognised:

Choice What it means
Apple dictation The default. Works straight away, but your speech may be sent to Apple.
Whisper base (local, fast) Recognised on your Mac. Downloads a small model the first time.
Whisper small (local, more accurate) Recognised on your Mac, more accurate, a little slower.

For anything sensitive, choose one of the Whisper options.

Dictation also works in Ask mode: press the microphone button next to the question box.

6. Ask a question

  1. Choose Ask in the sidebar on the left. In a narrow window there is no sidebar; use the Clean up / Ask switch at the top.
  2. Type a question in the box at the bottom and press Ask (or Return).
  3. The answer appears above. Ask a follow-up and the AI remembers the conversation so far.

Under each answer:

  • Stars let you rate it from one to five.
  • Copy puts the answer on the clipboard.
  • A line shows how long the answer took.

The conversation is kept automatically. Close the app, open it again, and it is still there.

New conversation, in the sidebar, clears the screen for a fresh topic. Before it does, it offers to add the conversation to Memory (see section 8).

The conversation you leave is not lost. It is listed in the sidebar under Conversations, by its first question. Click it to bring it back and carry on; the small × removes it from the list. The app keeps the last twelve.

7. Choose your AI

The menu at the top, labelled Clean up with or Answer with, lists every AI you can use. Change it whenever you like.

On this Mac: nothing is shared

  • Built-in Gemma 3 4B. Comes with the app after one download. Good for everyday clean-up and questions, and fits a Mac with 16 GB of memory.
  • Ollama models. If you have the free Ollama app installed, every model in it appears in the menu. Larger models give better answers but need more memory. Press the reload button next to the menu after installing a new one.

On the internet: your text is sent out

  • Claude Opus 5.5 (cloud). A much stronger AI that runs at Anthropic. What you send it leaves your Mac.

To use Claude:

  1. Create an API key at platform.claude.com. Anthropic charges for usage.
  2. In the app, press Add Claude API key at the top right and paste the key. It is stored in your Mac's Keychain.
  3. Choose Claude Opus 5.5 (cloud) in the menu.

The privacy bar and Offline mode

The coloured bar across the top always says where your words are going:

  • Green, "Private": the selected AI runs on this Mac.
  • Amber, "Online": the selected AI is Claude, and what you write is sent to Anthropic.

At the right of the bar is the Offline mode switch. While it is on, the app makes no internet connections at all:

  • Claude cannot be selected or used.
  • Nothing can be downloaded.
  • Apple dictation, which may send speech to Apple, is not started. Whisper still works if its model is already downloaded.
  • Help does not fetch its recordings.

The AIs on your Mac carry on working. Use Offline mode when you want to be certain, or to show someone that nothing leaves the computer.

The app never picks Claude for you. A simple rule: use a local AI for anything private, and Claude when the text is not sensitive and you want the best result.

8. Memory

By default each new conversation starts from nothing. Memory is a page of notes that carries over, so the AI already knows what matters when you start again.

To add a conversation to Memory:

  1. Press Memory in the sidebar.
  2. Press Add this conversation, using …. The selected AI rewrites the notes to include what was said.
  3. Read the notes. Edit anything that is wrong or that you do not want kept.
  4. Press Save.

From then on the notes are given to the AI with every question you ask. You can also type notes yourself, for example "I write in British English" or "Keep answers short".

Things to know:

  • Pressing New conversation offers to update Memory first, so you do not have to remember.
  • Nothing is saved until you press Save.
  • Memory is sent to whichever AI you ask. If you ask Claude, the notes go to Anthropic with the question.
  • To forget everything, open Memory, delete the text and press Save.

9. Save and open files

The app keeps your current text and conversation automatically. Files are for keeping more than one, or moving work somewhere else.

Writing

  • Save to file saves the cleaned-up text, or your own text if you have not cleaned it up yet.
  • Open file loads a text file into Your text.

Conversations

  • Save conversation saves the conversation as a Markdown file you can read in any text editor.
  • Open conversation loads one back so you can carry on where you stopped.

Memory

  • Inside the Memory box, Save a copy as a file and Load from a file do the same for the notes.

Every saved file records when it was saved, which AI was selected and how you were dictating. When you open a file, the app switches back to those settings. If that AI is not on the Mac you are using, the app says so and keeps the AI you have selected.

File names include the date and time, for example conversation 2026-11-21 1405.md.

10. Compare AIs

Listen: Comparing AIs, recorded by Nader Amir.

If you have more than one AI, you can find out which answers your kind of question best.

Ask the same question twice. After an answer, choose a different AI in the menu and press Ask the selected model the same question. Both answers appear together, and you can rate each with stars.

Compare blind. Type a question and press Compare blind. Pick two AIs. Their answers appear as Answer A and Answer B with the names hidden, so you judge the answer and not the brand. Press A is better, B is better or About the same, and the names are revealed.

Ask an AI to judge. Under a pair of answers, press Have the selected model judge. It reads the question and both answers and says which is better and why.

Run a question set. Press Question set in the sidebar, add the questions you care about, tick the AIs to ask, and press Run. Every AI answers every question. You can hide the names and have an AI judge each one.

See the results. Compare ratings, in the sidebar, shows, for each AI, how often you preferred it, how often the AI judge preferred it, and its average star rating.

11. Privacy at a glance

What Where it goes
Text you clean up or ask about, with a local AI Nowhere. It stays on your Mac.
Text you send to Claude, and your Memory notes with it To Anthropic, under your own API key
Speech, with Whisper Nowhere. Recognised on your Mac.
Speech, with Apple dictation May be sent to Apple
Drafts, conversations, Memory, ratings Stored on your Mac
Your Claude API key Your Mac's Keychain
Downloading an AI A normal download; your text is not sent

The makers of confidant·e receive nothing. The full policy is at https://nader938.github.io/confidant_e_docs/privacy.html.

12. Problems and fixes

"Offline mode is on, so…" You tried something that needs the internet. Turn off the Offline mode switch in the bar at the top, or carry on with an AI on your Mac.

No AI is listed, or the menu says "Choose…". Open the menu and pick Built-in Gemma 3 4B … · download.

A red bar says Ollama is not running. That only matters for Ollama models. Start the Ollama app and press the reload button, or choose the built-in AI.

The first answer is slow. The AI is loading into memory. Later runs are faster.

"That is too much text for the built-in model." The built-in AI reads a few thousand words at a time. Clean up a shorter piece, or use a larger Ollama model or Claude.

Dictation does nothing. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and allow confidant·e under Microphone and Speech Recognition.

The answer is wrong. It happens. AIs make mistakes and state them confidently. Check anything that matters, and do not treat an answer as professional advice.

Something else. Write to the address on the support page: https://nader938.github.io/confidant_e_docs/
